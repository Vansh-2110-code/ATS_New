1. ROLE PURPOSE

The Recruitment Executive is responsible for sourcing, screening, coordinating and closing suitable candidates against assigned IT and other approved requirements while maintaining the quality, speed and accuracy expected by the Company and its clients.

2. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

A. Candidate Sourcing

Source candidates through Naukri, LinkedIn, Shine, ATS database, referrals, job portals, social media and other approved sources.

Search candidates according to the exact job description and client requirement.

Maintain an active and relevant candidate pipeline.

Ensure resumes are submitted promptly against active requirements.

B. Candidate Screening

Conduct initial screening of candidates before submission.

Verify skills, experience, location, notice period, salary expectations and other job-specific requirements.

Ensure candidates genuinely match the requirement.

Avoid duplicate, irrelevant or inaccurate submissions.

Maintain accurate candidate information in the ATS.

C. Candidate Follow-up

Maintain regular communication with candidates.

Coordinate interviews and interview confirmations.

Follow up for interview attendance, feedback, offer acceptance and joining.

Maintain candidate engagement until joining and during the applicable retention period.

D. Client / Requirement Management

Understand assigned job descriptions clearly.

Clarify requirements with the Team Leader/Manager or client HR wherever necessary.

Work only on authorized job requisitions.

Follow Company instructions regarding priority requirements.

Ensure timely submission and follow-up.

E. ATS &amp; Reporting

All recruitment activity must be recorded in the Company's designated ATS/NEXORA system, including where applicable:

Candidate sourcing

Screening

Submissions

Interview status

Follow-ups

Dropouts
